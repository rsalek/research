03 · New field evidence

Indonesia confirms risk; balances cushion it

The most useful information since v1 came from national meteorology and official commodity balances. It strengthens the physical chain but shows why weather exposure and price exposure must remain separate.

Interpretation.

The climate and field gates now align for Indonesia. The crop and balance gates do not. Oil-palm research finds nonlinear effects across 0–23 month lags, so the decisive test is persistent rainfall deficits followed by 2027 output downgrades.

08 · Repeatable monitoring

Weekly scan; fortnightly decision note

A short, delta-only update is more useful than rewriting the thesis each week. The report should change only when a primary forecast, observed field variable, official balance or product structure changes.

Every weekNOAA diagnostics; BMKG/BoM rain and soil moisture; FEWS NET alerts; futures curves and two-way product quotes.
25–28 Aug 2026SARCOF-33 consensus outlook—the next important southern Africa maize/hydropower catalyst.
Late Aug / early SepICCO quarterly bulletin and West African main-crop pod/arrival evidence; do not pre-empt publication.
10–11 SepMPOB August statistics and USDA WASDE: test whether weather enters official balances.
MonthlyNOAA CPC on the second Thursday; IRI near the 20th; national crop agencies and issuer product pages.

Delta template for the next edition

  1. Forecast: probability, peak, persistence and disagreement.
  2. Field: observed rain/heat/soil moisture in named crop regions.
  3. Crop: yield-sensitive stage and credible condition/arrival data.
  4. Balance: production, exports, stocks and policy revisions.
  5. Market: curve, positioning, currency, spread and what is priced.
  6. Instrument: exact index, weights, structure, cost and availability.

Publish “no change” when evidence has not moved. That is a result, not a failure to update.

V2 bottom line

The climate view is stable; the regional evidence is becoming less hypothetical. Indonesia makes palm oil the clearest directional upgrade, but rising Malaysian output and stocks mean it is still a lagged 2027 production watch—not a confirmed near-term shortage.

Sugar has modestly higher asymmetry because Thailand begins from a weaker baseline. Coffee remains exposed but offset by Vietnam supply and an Arabica-versus-robusta product mismatch. Southern African maize risk has risen locally, while global grains and cocoa remain unconfirmed. The next high-value update is after SARCOF-33, the ICCO bulletin and early-September palm/WASDE data.
