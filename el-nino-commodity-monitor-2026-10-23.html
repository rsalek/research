Weekly El Niño commodity monitor | 23 October 2026

Observed Indonesian dryness strengthened. The commodity thesis did not.

BMKG's 23 October analysis is the only material new climate evidence since the 22 October monitor. It classified the ASO El Niño as very strong at +3.05°C, measured low rainfall across 80.81% of Indonesia and below-normal rainfall across 87.82%, and kept drought warnings active in parts of southern Sumatra and Kalimantan. This raises the lagged palm and robusta watch, but there is still no measured crop loss or new official balance deterioration. USDA's post-cutoff Rice Stocks release also does not change the broad-grain view.

Current stance. watchlist / wait for physical proof unchanged. Climate confidence is very high. Commodity-loss confidence remains low to medium. An observed rainfall deficit is not an inventory draw, production cut, export shortfall, or investment conclusion.

Regions and crop calendars

Where the forecast meets sensitive windows

Indonesia and Malaysia | palm oil

Harvest is year-round. Moisture stress affects flowering, fruit set and bunch formation before later output. The 23 October rain analysis strengthens the lagged watch. September's 3.511 Mt Malaysian stock cushion delays the scarcity test.

Vietnam and Indonesia | robusta

Vietnam's main harvest is starting. Dry weather may help picking at first. Persistent stress matters more for later flowering and fruit set. Current exports and London stocks still resist a scarcity call.

India and Thailand | sugar

India's October-start crushing season and Thailand's December-start season make cane weight, sucrose recovery, reservoirs and mill throughput the next proof points.

Southern Africa | maize

October to early January is the main planting window. Establishment rainfall is critical, especially for regional white maize. The 17.523 Mt commercial crop is the opening buffer.

Ghana and Côte d'Ivoire | cocoa

The October to March main crop is beginning. Arrivals, pod disease, bean quality and grindings must establish a balance change.

Global corn, wheat and rice

U.S. corn harvest execution remains the main near-term crop issue. Wheat, rice and the larger October stock estimates still offset a synchronized broad-grain shortage view.

Decision gates

Strengthen, weaken, invalidate

Strengthen

Indonesian deficits persist across palm and robusta production zones into crop-sensitive stages; MPOB output falls and stocks draw for several months; Vietnam arrivals or exports weaken; Asian cane recovery is cut; cocoa arrivals disappoint; South African planting is materially delayed; or several official balances tighten together.

Weaken

MPOB stocks stay above 3 Mt; robusta exports and certified stocks remain high; broad rain returns to southern Sumatra and Kalimantan; South African planting establishes normally; Asian sugar recovery holds; U.S. corn harvest catches up without condition loss; or cocoa arrivals improve.

Invalidate

A very strong El Niño passes without adverse target-region crop anomalies; official balances keep improving into early 2027; observed price moves are better explained by policy, logistics, foreign exchange, energy or positioning; or no UK-accessible instrument matches the physical channel.
