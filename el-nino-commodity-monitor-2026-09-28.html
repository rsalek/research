Change from 21 September

ENSO persistence strengthens. Physical proof remains selective.

IRI’s 21 September objective forecast, published after the previous report’s cut-off, is the clearest change: all 22 models keep Niño3.4 in the “very strong” category from SON through DJF, with El Niño probability at 100% through FMA 2027 before declining during boreal spring. BMKG’s 25 September update confirms that extreme rainless spells and high temperatures persist across parts of southern Indonesia. Neither development has yet produced a matching deterioration in palm output or stocks, robusta supply, sugar output, Southern African maize buffers, or cocoa balances.

Monitoring conclusion: climate conviction and the lagged Southeast Asian crop watch both strengthen, but the physical-balance gate does not. U.S. corn harvest reached 13% versus an 11% five-year average while crop condition stayed at 57% good/excellent, a modest execution offset rather than a balance reversal.

Not an investment recommendation. Climate sensitivity, observed crop-region weather, realized physical loss, market balance and instrument exposure remain separate tests.

Regions and crop calendars

Where the forecast meets sensitive windows

Indonesia / Malaysia · palm oil

Southern Sumatra and Kalimantan remain within BMKG’s broader dry/heat footprint. Current stress matters mainly through lagged bunch formation and 2027 yields. Malaysia’s latest output and stocks still require an observed downturn before the balance view changes.

Vietnam / Indonesia · robusta

Vietnam’s main harvest begins from October; dry weather can initially aid picking, while prolonged stress threatens later flowering. Current robusta exports and certified stocks still resist a realized-deficit conclusion.

India / Thailand · sugar

India’s monsoon tail overlaps cane growth and sucrose accumulation; Thailand approaches crushing. Reservoirs, cane weight, recovery rates, area and ethanol policy—not rainfall maps alone—must confirm the physical effect.

South Africa · white/yellow maize

October–December planting and establishment remain the most immediate forecast-to-crop overlap. The record 2026 crop is the opening buffer. White maize is regionally specific and not cleanly represented by global corn futures.

Ghana / Côte d’Ivoire · cocoa

The October–March main crop is approaching. Rainfall, pod counts, disease and arrivals matter, but ICCO still withholds the current-season production and grinding estimates needed for a balance conclusion.

Global corn / wheat / rice

U.S. corn harvest is ahead of the five-year pace while condition is unchanged. Grain Stocks on 30 September is the next hard inventory checkpoint; wheat and rice continue to buffer broad-basket exposure.

Decision gates

Strengthen, weaken, invalidate

Strengthen

Indonesia dryness persists into later bunch formation/flowering and MPOB yields/output fall; robusta arrivals or exports tighten; South African planting rains fail and buffers erode; cane recovery falls; U.S. stocks or yield are cut; West African cocoa arrivals disappoint.

Weaken

Rain normalises before sensitive stages; the IOD remains neutral; palm output/stocks stay ample; Vietnam harvest and robusta exports remain strong; South African planting establishes normally; cane estimates hold; corn stocks/yield improve; cocoa arrivals recover.

Invalidate

An exceptionally strong El Niño persists without adverse anomalies in the target crop regions; official balances improve despite weather; observed moves are explained by policy, FX, energy, freight or positioning; or the available product tracks a different contract than the physical thesis.
